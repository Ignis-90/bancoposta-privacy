Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Who this policy concerns

PJ BancoPosta (the "Application") is a private application owned and used exclusively by Piergiorgio Panzini (the "Owner"). It processes data from a single bank account: the Owner's own personal BancoPosta account held with Poste Italiane. The Application has no other users and does not collect data about any other person, except for the counterparty details that naturally appear in the Owner's own transactions.

2. Current status

The Application is under development. At the date above:

  • In progress: configuration of the bank connection through Enable Banking.
  • Planned, not yet operational: local storage of the retrieved data on the Owner's computer, and the analysis and categorisation of spending described below.

This policy will be updated if the way data is handled changes.

3. How bank data is accessed

Bank data is accessed through Enable Banking, a third party Open Banking provider, using the account information interfaces that banks make available under the EU Payment Services Directive (PSD2). Access is only possible after the Owner gives explicit consent and authenticates directly with the bank. Access is read only.

4. Data that may be processed

  • Account identifiers and account balances
  • Transactions: dates, amounts, currency and descriptions
  • Counterparty information contained in transactions, such as the name of a merchant or of the other party

The Application does not request or store the Owner's online banking credentials. Authentication takes place with the bank.

5. Purpose of use

Data is used solely to monitor, categorise and analyse the Owner's personal spending. It is not sold, shared for marketing, used for profiling of third parties or used for any commercial purpose.

6. Third parties involved

Bank data does not stay only on the Owner's computer. It passes through, or may be processed by, the following providers:

  • Poste Italiane, the bank that holds the account and provides the data.
  • Enable Banking, which connects to the bank and transmits the data to the Application. Its handling of data is governed by its own terms and privacy policy, available at enablebanking.com.
  • Anthropic (planned). The Owner intends to analyse and categorise transactions with Claude, an AI assistant provided by Anthropic. When this happens, the transaction data included in a request is sent to Anthropic's servers for processing, under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy, available at anthropic.com.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains only these informational pages and does not receive or store any bank data.

7. Storage and deletion

It is planned that retrieved data will be stored locally on the Owner's personal computer. Data will be kept until the Owner decides to delete it; there is no fixed retention period. The Owner can delete the locally stored data at any time.

Data held by Enable Banking, Poste Italiane or Anthropic is retained according to each provider's own policies and is outside the Application's control.

8. Revoking access

The bank authorisation can be revoked at any time by the Owner, through Enable Banking or through the bank. Bank authorisations under PSD2 also expire after the period set by the bank and must be renewed by the Owner to continue. After revocation or expiry, the Application can no longer retrieve new data.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Application is developed. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
