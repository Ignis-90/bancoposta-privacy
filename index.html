PJ BancoPosta

A private, personal application. Not a commercial service.

PJ BancoPosta is a personal tool built and used by a single individual, Piergiorgio Panzini, to read balances and transactions from his own BancoPosta account. Its purpose is to monitor, categorise and analyse his personal spending.

Bank data is accessed through Enable Banking, a third party Open Banking provider, using the account information APIs made available under the EU Payment Services Directive (PSD2). Access is read only and requires the account holder's explicit consent.

The application has no payment, transfer or other money movement features, is not offered to anyone else, and does not accept users or customers.

Status: the application is under development. The bank connection is being configured; the local storage and analysis components described in the policies are planned and not yet operational.
